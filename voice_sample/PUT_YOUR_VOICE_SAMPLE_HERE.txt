ONE-TIME VOICE CLONE SAMPLE — do this once, then every reel uses your voice.

Drop ONE clean recording of your voice in this folder:
  - 1 to 5 minutes, just you talking, minimal background noise/music.
  - Audio (.wav .mp3 .m4a .flac) or video (.mp4 .mov) both work.

Then create the clone (one time), in ElevenLabs:
    1. Go to  https://elevenlabs.io/app/voice-lab
       -> Add Voice -> Instant Voice Clone -> upload the sample from this folder
       -> name it and save.
    2. Get an API key: https://elevenlabs.io/app/settings/api-keys
       and put it in .env as  ELEVEN_API_KEY=...  (see .env.example).
    3. Come back here and run:
         python elevenlabs_voice.py --check
       It verifies your key and shows your voices with their ids.
    4. Put your clone's id in .env as  ELEVEN_VOICE_ID=...

After that, quickVid defaults to your cloned voice for every voiceover — you
never touch this folder again. Keep the sample here for reference.

You can delete this note once your clone is set up.
