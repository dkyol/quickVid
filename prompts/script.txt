Google just showed off something big. Pipelines that build media on their own. Feed in text, audio, images, and video, and it blends everything into one piece, effortlessly. No manual editing, no timeline. Then it runs a self correcting loop. It checks its own work, catches the rough spots, and fixes them, automatically, again and again, until the result is ready. Just drop in your raw content. Everything else, it handles itself.
