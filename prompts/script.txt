Welcome to the front nine at Langston Golf Course, home to Washington D.C.'s historic National Links Trust. Hole one opens with a 465-yard par five. Hole two tightens to a 376-yard par four, then hole three stretches out to 492 yards, another par five. The par-three fourth tests your irons at 157. Holes five through seven wind through mid-length par fours: 350, 358, and 328 yards. The par-three eighth plays 194, before the par-four ninth closes the side at 388. Thirty-six strokes to par, and a piece of golf history in every step.
