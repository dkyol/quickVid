Renditions Golf Course, Davidsonville, Maryland — nine replica holes from major championship venues, including Amen Corner.

Hole one: 361 yards, replicating The Country Club's iconic 17th. Hole two: a crisp 137-yard par three modeled on Inverness. Hole three channels Southern Hills' 17th at just 306 yards. Hole four borrows Royal Lytham & St. Annes' demanding 16th. Hole five stretches 499 yards, echoing Royal Birkdale's 15th. Hole six opens Amen Corner — Augusta National's treacherous 11th. Hole seven: Augusta's legendary 12th, Golden Bell, over Rae's Creek. Hole eight closes Amen Corner with Augusta's par-five 13th, Azalea. Hole nine finishes strong at Oakland Hills' brutal 16th, 377 yards.
